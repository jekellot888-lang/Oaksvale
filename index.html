Kampala, Uganda

A full-service firm for individuals, businesses, financial institutions, investors and public and private organisations. We combine sound legal knowledge with an understanding of the commercial, institutional and practical realities our clients face.

Vision

To build a respected and enduring regional law firm, recognised for excellence in legal practice, strong advocacy, integrity, innovation and practical solutions.

Mission

To provide high-quality, strategic and accessible legal services that protect our clients' interests, resolve disputes effectively and support sustainable personal, commercial and institutional growth.

Uganda & Kenya

Commerce, investment and legal practice in East Africa are increasingly integrated. Our team includes advocates with professional capacity across Uganda and Kenya, so matters with a cross-border dimension stay with one firm.

  • Cross-border commercial transactions
  • Corporate and investment advisory
  • Cross-border dispute resolution
  • Debt recovery and commercial litigation
  • Employment matters
  • Property and investment transactions
  • Coordinated legal support across Uganda and Kenya

Where a matter needs representation or advice in another jurisdiction, the Firm works within the applicable professional, regulatory and licensing requirements.
